# Positioning Notes

## ICP

The initial ICP is a manual money thinker: freelancers, indie builders, privacy-conscious note users, and spreadsheet-light users who want a place to capture and reason about cashflow without a full finance app.

They do not want bank sync as the first feature. They want speed, control, editability, and a record they can understand later.

## JTBD

When I have messy money context in my head, voice, receipt photos, or notes, I want AI to turn it into clean notebook facts so I can calculate only the questions I choose and keep the reasoning trail.

## Category

Do not position as "AI expense tracker." That category is crowded and implies transaction tables, budgets, dashboards, and automatic categorization.

Better category candidates:

- AI financial notebook
- AI money notebook
- notebook calculator for personal finance
- AI Composer for money notes

## One-line

MonoSpend is an AI financial notebook: speak, paste, or snap messy money context, and it becomes editable notebook lines with calculation notes when you ask.

## Differentiation

- Against Soulver: finance-specific AI Composer and money notebook workflows, not a general calculator notebook.
- Against Apple Notes: structured right-column results and deterministic calculation notes.
- Against AI expense apps: canonical notebook lines instead of transaction tables and dashboards.
- Against hledger: approachable notebook UI instead of technical accounting syntax.
- Against spreadsheets: faster capture and less grid management.

## Not-for

Not for users who primarily want bank sync, investment tracking, automatic categorization dashboards, tax reports, or AI financial advice.

Not for users who expect the app to passively manage money without confirmation.

## Claims

Safe claims:

- editable notebook lines;
- user-triggered calculation notes;
- local-first prototype;
- exportable notebook;
- AI-assisted capture.

Claims needing verification:

- better retention than expense apps;
- users prefer calculation notes over automatic totals;
- privacy tolerance for cloud AI;
- willingness to pay.
