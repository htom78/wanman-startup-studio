# Landing Page Experiment

## First viewport

Headline:

> Your AI money notebook.

Subhead:

> Speak, paste, or snap messy money context. MonoSpend turns it into editable notebook lines, then saves calculations as notes only when you ask.

Primary CTA:

> Try the notebook prototype

Secondary CTA:

> See examples

Hero visual:

Use the current paper notebook prototype, showing:

```text
lunch = $55.00 + 25% tip        -$68.75
calculate remaining             $1,486.25
```

## CTA

CTA should not promise a finished finance app. Use validation language:

- "Try the notebook prototype"
- "Test with 5 money notes"
- "Join the one-week notebook test"

## Smoke test

Test three positioning variants:

1. AI financial notebook
2. AI money notebook
3. Soulver-style notebook for personal finance

Measure:

- CTA click rate;
- whether users understand calculation notes;
- whether users volunteer real examples;
- whether users prefer notebook lines or transaction rows.

## Claims

Allowed:

- AI Composer writes editable lines.
- Calculations are user-triggered notes.
- Works as a local prototype.
- Export supported.

Do not claim:

- fully offline AI;
- financial advice;
- bank-level security;
- guaranteed OCR accuracy;
- automatic savings.

## Do not build

Do not build a polished marketing site before the one-week validation test. The landing page should recruit testers and explain the interaction, not sell a mature finance app.
