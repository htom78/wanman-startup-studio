# Competitor Map

## Category map

MonoSpend AI Notebook sits between four categories:

- notepad calculators: Soulver, Numi-style workflows;
- note apps: Apple Notes, Notion, Obsidian;
- AI expense trackers: Duly, HeyJerni, Dengii, Yavo, Yomio, Receiptix;
- plain-text accounting: hledger, Ledger, Beancount.

The opportunity is not to beat every category. It is to combine the approachable notebook mental model, AI capture, and deterministic money calculations in one narrow workflow.

## Direct competitors

AI expense trackers are the most dangerous if MonoSpend is described as capture software. Duly claims voice AI and receipt scanning. HeyJerni claims offline/on-device AI for voice and receipt scans. Dengii claims one phrase can become multiple expense records.

These validate capture demand but make capture a weak standalone wedge.

## Adjacent alternatives

Soulver validates the two-column calculation notebook but is not finance-specific and expects the user to write expressions. Apple Notes validates the note-taking canvas but lacks native financial parsing and calculation columns. hledger validates owned text records but is too technical for casual users.

## Manual alternatives

Manual alternatives include calculator plus Notes, spreadsheet tabs, receipt photos, and ChatGPT parsing. These are good enough unless MonoSpend is faster and more pleasant.

## Opportunity gaps

- AI-generated canonical notebook lines rather than transaction rows.
- Calculation answers saved as user-triggered notes.
- Finance-specific examples: tips, splits, subscriptions, runway, recurring income, total cost.
- Local-first exportable notebook.

## Risks

- The product may be perceived as a skin over Soulver.
- The product may be perceived as a weaker AI expense tracker.
- The notebook style may not matter to mainstream users.
- Privacy expectations may exceed prototype capability.
