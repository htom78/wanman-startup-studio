# Product Research Source Index

## Source policy

Use current product pages and app listings as evidence of claimed capabilities. They do not prove traction or retention. Mark all adoption, revenue, accuracy, and pricing comparisons as `needs verification`.

## Unsupported claims

- Users will prefer ledger output over a transaction table.
- Cloud AI will be acceptable if the saved ledger stays local.
- One-time pricing is viable with AI costs.
- A narrow MVP can convert testers into retained users.
- All of the above are `needs verification`.
