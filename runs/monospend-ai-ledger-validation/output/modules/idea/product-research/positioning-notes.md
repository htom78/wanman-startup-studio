# Product Research Positioning Notes

## ICP

First ICP: privacy-conscious manual trackers who want faster capture but still want to own and inspect their money records. They may dislike bank sync, dislike finance dashboards, and already use notes, spreadsheets, or lightweight trackers.

Exclude bank-sync users, dashboard-heavy budgeters, investors, shared household budgeters, and people who want an AI financial advisor.

## JTBD

When I spend money and do not want to fill out a form, help me speak, snap, or type the purchase and turn it into a clean ledger line I can trust, edit, and export.

## Category

Best category: AI notepad ledger.

Secondary category: AI capture for personal finance notes.

Avoid primary category: AI expense tracker.

## One-line

MonoSpend turns voice, receipts, and messy money notes into a private notepad ledger you can read, edit, and export.

## Differentiation

- AI output is visible ledger text.
- User confirmation is part of the workflow.
- Records remain exportable and durable.
- Privacy promise is grounded in local ledger ownership.
- Future on-device AI can strengthen the claim without changing the data model.

## Not-for

Not for users who want automatic bank import, full financial dashboards, AI advice, shared wallets, investment tracking, or completely hands-off bookkeeping.

## Assumptions

- Ledger ownership is meaningful to enough users.
- AI capture is a strong enough habit trigger.
- A cloud-assisted v0 can be acceptable if the privacy copy is honest.
- The MVP can test value before building native app depth.
