# Competitor Threat Map

## Direct

Direct competitors now include AI expense trackers with voice and photo capture:

- Papirer: voice input, receipt scanner, natural text, PDF receipts, and AI categorization.
- HeyJerni: privacy-first offline AI expense tracker with voice input and receipt scan.
- Duly AI: voice AI, receipt scanning, bill splitting, and AI insights.
- Qaizo: voice, receipt scanner, smart budgeting, and AI chat.
- Costline: voice and photo input for expense tracking.
- Receeto: offline AI receipt scanner and expense tracker.
- SmartWalt, Spendspace, Kasso, Scanomy, WalletWhisper, and Yavo: variations on voice, photo, AI categorization, budgets, and insights.

This means AI capture cannot be the whole differentiation.

## Adjacent

Adjacent competitors:

- Natural-language trackers such as Dengii, MonAi, and SPENDI.
- Privacy-first offline trackers such as Stroberi and Expense Trail.
- Plain-text accounting tools such as hledger, Ledger, and Beancount.
- Manual notes, spreadsheets, receipt folders, and reimbursement workflows.

Plain-text accounting is important because it proves some users value owned text records, but it is too complex for many mainstream users.

## Manual

Manual substitutes remain strong:

- Snap a receipt and leave it in Photos.
- Type a spending note into Apple Notes.
- Put expenses in a spreadsheet later.
- Keep a monthly cashflow note.
- Use a calculator after the fact.

MonoSpend wins only if AI makes capture easier and the ledger output makes the record more trustworthy than a hidden database.

## Pricing

Competitor pricing varies and is `needs verification`. The old MonoSpend $4.99 one-time Pro pricing may be too low if AI processing has recurring cost. A safer early model may be free local ledger plus limited AI capture, paid capture pack, or bring-your-own-key for privacy/cost control.

## Differentiation

The strongest wedge:

> Speak it, snap it, or type it. MonoSpend turns it into a clean notepad ledger you own.

Differentiators:

- AI extraction produces a visible ledger line, not just a hidden transaction.
- User confirms or edits before save.
- Ledger remains readable, searchable, exportable, and calculation-friendly.
- Privacy is framed as local storage and explicit AI consent, not vague "AI magic."
- Future on-device AI can strengthen the privacy promise.

## Threat summary

The biggest threat is commodity AI capture. Many apps already do voice and receipt scanning. MonoSpend must make the user-owned ledger the product's center of gravity.
