## Evidence policy

Competitor pages prove category activity and claimed features, not traction. Any claims about users, revenue, accuracy, model quality, or retention are `needs verification`.

The Idea Gate can treat "AI capture is crowded" as a strong qualitative finding and "editable notepad ledger is differentiated" as an unproven but plausible hypothesis.
