## Evidence policy

Facts from product pages can describe what products claim. They do not prove usage, retention, revenue, or customer satisfaction.

Competitor feature details, pricing, traffic, market size, user ratings, and willingness to pay are `needs verification`.

The Idea Gate should treat "privacy/offline is crowded" as a strong inference from observed competitor density, not as a quantified market fact.
