# Product Research Source Index

## Source policy

Use official product, pricing, docs, company, or reputable market sources first. When the research is based on local reasoning or the Founder Playbook, label it as such. Any claim about current features, pricing, market size, or popularity must be treated as `needs verification` until checked live.
