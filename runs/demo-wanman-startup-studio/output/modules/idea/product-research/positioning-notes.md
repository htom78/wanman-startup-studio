# Product Research Positioning Notes

## ICP

First ICP: solo technical founders or two-person AI-native teams who already use AI coding/research tools, work comfortably with local files, and are deciding whether a new product idea deserves MVP work.

Exclude nontechnical founders who want a hosted dashboard immediately, teams seeking distribution help, and founders who mainly need fundraising advice rather than validation discipline.

## JTBD

When I have a startup idea and AI tools make it easy to build, help me slow down just enough to prove whether the problem is real, understand alternatives, and decide what the smallest MVP should test.

## Category

Evidence-first startup operating system. A secondary category is stage-gated AI founder workflow. Avoid generic "AI startup studio" positioning because it sounds like automatic company creation.

## One-line

Wanman Startup Studio helps AI-native founders turn raw startup ideas into explicit evidence gates before they spend time building the wrong MVP.

## Differentiation

- Executable stage gates instead of passive templates.
- Required disconfirming evidence before MVP planning.
- Local Markdown/JSON artifacts that agents and humans can both inspect.
- Optional wanman orchestration without depending on it for reliability.
- Product research becomes Idea Gate evidence, not a vanity landing page pipeline.

## Not-for

Not for founders who want a one-click company generator, automated fundraising, a polished website builder, or a hosted CRM on day one. Not for teams that are already in Launch or Scale and mainly need GTM operations.

## Assumptions

- Technical founders are willing to run a local workflow if it prevents wasted build time.
- The phrase "stage-gated" communicates discipline rather than bureaucracy.
- Product research plus a smoke-test plan is enough to improve the Idea Gate decision.
- Landing page creation should remain optional until interviews confirm the value of testing the promise publicly.
