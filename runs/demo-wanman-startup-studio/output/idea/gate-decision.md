# Idea Gate Decision

Decision: ALLOW_MVP

## Evidence

The problem is coherent enough to proceed to MVP Gate because the Founder Playbook framing creates a clear new failure mode: AI lets founders research, write, and build faster, which increases the need for orchestration and stage discipline. The proposed product directly addresses that failure mode by forcing a build/no-build decision before MVP work starts.

The target customer is also reachable for a v0: solo technical founders and small AI-native teams already using local tools, Markdown, and AI coding agents. This segment can evaluate a file-based prototype without requiring a hosted product.

The strongest product wedge is not generic AI startup advice. It is the explicit gate that blocks MVP artifacts unless the Idea Gate says `ALLOW_MVP`.

## Risks

- Customer willingness to pay is not proven.
- Interview evidence is still missing.
- The workflow may feel like process overhead.
- A black-box hosted competitor could feel easier for nontechnical founders.
- The local-first workflow may narrow the first market.

## Next step

Proceed to MVP Gate, but only for a narrow MVP: local runs, role packs, validators, and demo artifacts. Do not build Launch or Scale automation yet. The MVP Gate must define scope, architecture, measurement, and security before any product UI or hosted service work.

## Conditions

The MVP should be considered invalid if interviews do not confirm that founders have recently overbuilt, lost validation context, or needed a stronger gate before starting MVP work.
