# Customer Discovery Plan

## Recruiting

Start with ten founder/operators who have recently used AI coding or research tools to evaluate a startup idea. Prioritize people who have shipped at least one prototype, killed at least one idea, or maintained multiple AI project chats.

Recruiting channels:

- Personal network of technical founders and indie hackers.
- Small founder communities where people discuss AI coding workflows.
- Product consultants or agency strategists who repeatedly validate new ideas.
- Existing users of local AI development tools who are comfortable with file-based workflows.

Avoid recruiting only people who already like agent systems. The first round should include skeptical founders who prefer a notebook, spreadsheet, or direct customer calls.

## Interview questions

1. Tell me about the last startup or product idea you evaluated. What happened from idea to build decision?
2. Where did you keep research, customer notes, competitor notes, and scope decisions?
3. When did you decide it was time to build something? What evidence did you have?
4. Have AI tools made it easier for you to build too early?
5. What part of idea validation feels slow, repetitive, or easy to skip?
6. What would make you trust a stage gate that says "do not build yet"?
7. What would you refuse to put into a local startup operating system?
8. If this workflow produced a strong `BLOCK` or `PIVOT` recommendation, what would you do next?
9. What artifacts would you want before involving a cofounder, advisor, or customer?
10. How would you measure whether this workflow saved you time or prevented a bad build?

## Leading questions

Do not ask "Would you use an AI startup studio?" because it invites politeness. Do not pitch wanman, agents, or automation early. The interview should first reconstruct the customer's real workflow and pain.

Better prompts:

- "Show me the notes or files from the last idea you evaluated."
- "Where did the decision become messy?"
- "What did you build that you later regretted building?"

## Evidence capture

For each interview, capture:

- Customer segment and context.
- Recent concrete workflow.
- Trigger that caused the idea evaluation.
- Existing tools and manual alternatives.
- Evidence used for build/no-build decision.
- Moment where AI accelerated useful work.
- Moment where AI created noise, scope creep, or false confidence.
- Exact words that describe pain, urgency, and trust.

## Success threshold

Proceed toward MVP only if at least five of ten interviews show a repeated workflow breakdown around premature building, scattered validation context, or lack of explicit gate decisions. If interviews show founders mainly need generic templates, coaching, or customer access, the idea should pivot.
