# Competitor Threat Map

## Direct

Direct competitors are products or workflows that promise startup idea validation, market research, or AI-assisted startup planning.

- Validator-style AI idea feedback tools: fast, accessible, but often shallow and hard to audit.
- Landing page generators such as Mixo-style tools: useful for smoke tests, but they may encourage public-facing output before customer discovery.
- AI research wrappers: can gather market and competitor notes, but usually do not enforce stage gates.

Threat: these tools are easier to understand and may feel more complete because they produce polished artifacts quickly.

## Adjacent

Adjacent alternatives include:

- ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Codex project threads.
- Notion startup templates.
- YC Startup School style worksheets and founder education resources.
- Product discovery frameworks used by consultants and PMs.
- Agent orchestration frameworks that can be configured into a similar workflow.

Threat: customers may already have enough process in these tools and may not want another layer.

## Manual

Manual alternatives are strong:

- Founder notebook plus customer calls.
- Spreadsheet with interview notes and competitor rows.
- A shared folder of Markdown documents.
- Advisor or cofounder review.
- A lightweight landing page plus direct outreach.

Threat: serious founders may prefer the flexibility of manual judgement and reject a formal gate unless it demonstrably saves time or improves decisions.

## Pricing

Pricing is `needs verification`. Early buyer willingness could range from free/open-source usage to a paid template/workflow package or a hosted team product. The first paid hypothesis should not assume SaaS pricing. A one-time startup validation kit or service-assisted workflow may be easier to sell first.

## Differentiation

The clearest differentiation is not "AI research" or "startup generator." The wedge is:

> A local, evidence-first operating system that blocks MVP work until the Idea Gate is explicit.

Differentiators:

- Stage-gated file workflow rather than open-ended chat.
- Required disconfirming evidence.
- Explicit promotion script from Idea to MVP.
- Durable context for future agents.
- Founder remains decision-maker.

## Threat summary

The biggest competitive threat is that customers may perceive this as process overhead. The product must prove that a gated workflow prevents wasted build cycles faster than a founder can assemble the same process manually.
