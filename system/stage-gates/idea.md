# Idea Gate

## Purpose

Decide whether the startup idea deserves MVP work. This gate protects the founder from mistaking building for validation.

## Required Evidence

- Problem is specific, recurring, and attached to a reachable customer segment.
- Discovery plan can produce direct customer evidence.
- Competitor and manual alternatives are understood.
- Strong counterarguments are documented.
- The solution concept is lightweight and explicitly avoids unnecessary build work.
- Claims are sourced, tagged, or marked `needs verification`.

## Legal Decisions

- `Decision: ALLOW_MVP`
- `Decision: PIVOT`
- `Decision: BLOCK`

## Pass Standard

Use `ALLOW_MVP` only when the evidence explains who has the problem, why it matters now, what current alternatives fail to solve, and what the first MVP should test.
